      Digital Privacy and Security Awareness Guide

      Follow these practical tips to protect your personal information and improve your digital security.

      1. Passwords and Account Security

      • Use a unique password for each important account.
      • Consider using a reputable password manager.
      • Enable multifactor authentication where available.
      • Never share verification codes with other people.

      2. Smartphone and App Privacy

      • Use a screen lock on your device.
      • Install operating system and app updates promptly.
      • Review camera, microphone, contacts and location permissions.
      • Install apps from trusted sources and remove apps you no longer use.

      3. Phishing and Online Scams

      • Be cautious of unexpected messages that demand urgent action.
      • Check website addresses before signing in.
      • Do not open suspicious links or attachments.
      • Verify unusual payment or account requests using an official contact method.

      4. Personal Information and Privacy

      • Limit personal information shared publicly on social media.
      • Review who can see your posts and profile information.
      • Turn off unnecessary location sharing.
      • Check privacy settings before using a new app or online service.

      5. Backups and Incident Response

      • Back up important files and photos regularly.
      • Check that you can restore your backups.
      • If an account is compromised, use a trusted device to secure it.
      • Change affected passwords, review recovery details and sign out unknown sessions where possible.
      • Contact the relevant service provider or bank if financial information may be at risk.

      Remember: No single security measure prevents every threat. Review your practices regularly and seek help when needed.
